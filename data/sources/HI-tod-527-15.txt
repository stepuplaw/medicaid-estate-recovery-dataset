 HRS 

      [§527‑ 15]  Liability for creditor 
claims and statutory allowances.  (a)  To the extent the transferor's 
probate estate is insufficient to satisfy an allowed claim against the estate 
or a statutory allowance to a surviving spouse or child, the estate may enforce 
the liability against the subject property transferred at the transferor's 
death by a transfer on death deed.

      (b)  If more than one property is transferred 
by one or more transfer on death deeds, the liability under subsection (a) 
shall be apportioned among the subject properties in proportion to their 
respective net values at the time of the transferor's death.

      (c)  A proceeding to enforce liability under 
this section shall be commenced not later than eighteen months after the 
transferor's death. [L 2011, c 173, pt of §1]
