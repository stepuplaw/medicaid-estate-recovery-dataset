 Title 89 - SOCIAL SERVICES 

 Ill. Admin. Code tit. 89, pt. 102 - RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES 

 Ill. Admin. Code tit. 89, § 102.200 - Recovery of Assistance

 Ill. Admin. Code tit. 89, § 102.200 - Recovery of Assistance 

 State Regulations 

 Compare

 a) By
 means of claims against the estates of deceased recipients and the estates of
 their deceased spouses and liens against recipients' real property interests,
 the Department has a statutory right to recover assistance provided to or in
 behalf of recipients according to the terms prescribed in this
 Section.

 b) The Department shall
 effect its recoveries by one of the following actions: 
 1) Acceptance of an amount, as settlement,
 equal to the estimated amount which would be collected if the estate were
 administered or the lien foreclosed;

 2) Administration of the estate; or

 3) Foreclosure of the lien.

 c) When the Department has both an
 estate claim and a real property lien, collection of the claim and lien shall
 be by one action.
