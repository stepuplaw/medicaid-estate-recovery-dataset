SB-315 (Roth), Revocable transfer on death deeds
Chapter Number: 215 (Stats. 2021, ch. 215)
Date Approved by Governor: September 22, 2021; filed with Secretary of State September 22, 2021
Legislative Counsel's Digest (quoted by WebFetch): "Existing law makes these provisions inoperative on January 1, 2022. This bill would revise and recast those provisions, and instead make them operative until January 1, 2032."
Other points reported: beneficiary liability for unsecured debts through sections on personal liability and abatement rules; "Common Questions" note the TOD deed does not affect Medi-Cal eligibility; California Law Revision Commission to study and report by January 1, 2031.

SB-1305 (Roth), 2019-2020 session
Chapter Number: 238 (Stats. 2020, ch. 238)
Date Approved by Governor: September 28, 2020; filed with Secretary of State September 28, 2020
Digest: "This bill would extend the operative date of those provisions until January 1, 2022."

Original act: AB 139 (Gatto, Ch. 293, Stats. 2015), five-year pilot set to expire January 1, 2021 (per Senate Judiciary Committee analysis of SB 1305, saved as CA-tod-sb1305-analysis.txt). Prob. Code Sec. 5600(a): part applies to transferors who die on or after January 1, 2016.
