 Medicaid Estate Recovery

 Medicaid Estate Recovery

 Medicaid estate recovery is required by federal and state law. When a Medicaid member dies, their property may be used to repay the Medicaid benefits they received.
 HHS has a preferred Medicaid claim against the property of a deceased Medicaid member:
 Who was age 55 or older when they received Medicaid services; or
 Who was permanently institutionalized regardless of age and received a Notice of Permanent Institutionalization; or
 Upon the death of the surviving spouse, minor child, or blind or disabled child of any age of the Medicaid member.
 Medicaid will not pursue estate recovery activities during:
 Lifetime of a Medicaid member's surviving spouse; or
 While there is a surviving child who is under the age of 21; or
 While there is a surviving child of any age who is blind or permanently and totally disabled. 
 For more information see: Estate Recovery Policy Manual
