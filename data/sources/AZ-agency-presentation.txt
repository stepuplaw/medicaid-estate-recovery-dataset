Arizona Medicaid Estate Recovery Program

Jakenna Lebsock
Assistant Director, Division of Health Care Services
                        Program Overview
●   Federally required program
●   Seeks to recover payments AHCCCS has made on behalf of
    members enrolled in the Arizona Long Term Care System
    (ALTCS)
     ○   Individuals are made aware that AHCCCS may collect the cost of
         care for services from their estate or real property during the
         enrollment process
●   Files claims and liens on member estates and real property - up
    to the amount of ALTCS payments made
● Can delay/waiver collection if the member or heir has a reasonable
    statutory exemption or verified undue hardship
