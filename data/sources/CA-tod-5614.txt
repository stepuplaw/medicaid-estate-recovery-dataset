 § 5614

Probate Code section 5614

 (a)
“Revocable transfer on death deed” means an instrument created pursuant to this part that does all of the following: 
 (1)
Makes a donative transfer of real property to a named beneficiary. 
 (2)
Operates on the transferor’s death. 
 (3)
Remains revocable until the transferor’s death. 
 (b)
A revocable transfer on death deed may also be known as a “revocable TOD deed.” 
 (c)
A revocable transfer on death deed may be used to transfer real property even if ownership of the property is not typically evidenced or transferred by use of a deed.
