 About Recoveries

 There are three types of recoveries handled by the Office of the Medicaid Inspector General (OMIG). Provider recoveries and recoupment through the OMIG recoveries team, and recipient recoveries through the MER team.

 Provider Recoveries and Recoupment

 Under the oversight of the OMIG Recoveries team, this function recovers payments from providers in cases involving fraud, waste, or abuse, as referred by the   Surveillance & Utilization Review (SUR) Unit. 

 Learn more about Recoveries 

 Provider Exclusions, Sanctions & Press Releases 

 Medicaid Estate Recovery (MER)

 MER recovers Medicaid funds paid on behalf of a recipient by collecting from the recipient's estate/assets after their death.
