 Chapter 76 

 76-3419.

 Certain contracts; requirements.

 A contract
to make a transfer on death deed, or not to revoke a transfer on death deed,
can be established only by a writing evidencing the contract signed by the
transferor after January 1, 2013.

 Source

 Laws 2012, LB536, § 19.
