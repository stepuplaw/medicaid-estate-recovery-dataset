New Mexico Statutes Sec. 27-2A-3: Definitions

As used in the Medicaid Estate Recovery Act:

A. "department" means the human services [health care authority] department;

B. "estate" means real and personal property and other assets of the individual subject to probate or administration pursuant to the provisions of the Uniform Probate Code; and

C. "medical assistance" means amounts paid by the department as medical assistance pursuant to Title XIX of the Social Security Act.

References: NMSA 1978, Sec. 45-1-101 et seq. (Uniform Probate Code); 42 U.S.C.A. Sec. 1396 et seq.
