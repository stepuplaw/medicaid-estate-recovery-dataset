State of Missouri v. Violet J. Knight and Tommy Jones (In re Estate of Wallace G. Jones)
Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District, No. WD 69310, decided January 13, 2009.
(The revisor's annotation to RSMo 461.300 cites this as In re Estate of Jones, 280 S.W.3d 647, marked "Mo.banc".)

Facts: Wallace G. Jones, age 92, received Medicaid nursing home benefits. He died November 9, 2003. His home, his only asset, passed to his children by a beneficiary deed filed January 7, 2000. The State sought $22,226.24 in Medicaid expenses from this nonprobate transfer.

Holding (quoted by WebFetch): "the State may utilize section 461.300 to bring an action for accounting in the context of Medicaid estate recovery."
On 473.398: "the state legislature has obviously chosen to deem section 461.300 accounting actions as proceedings under the probate code," so section 473.398's language "does not preclude the use of section 461.300 to recover Medicaid benefits."
