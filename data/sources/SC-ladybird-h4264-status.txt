Summary: SC Enhanced Life Estate Deed Act
   A BILL TO AMEND THE SOUTH CAROLINA CODE OF LAWS BY ENACTING THE "SOUTH CAROLINA ENHANCED
LIFE ESTATE DEED ACT" BY ADDING PART 5 TO ARTICLE 6, TITLE 62, SO AS TO ESTABLISH AN ENHANCED LIFE
ESTATE DEED AND TO PROVIDE FOR PROCESSES AND PROCEDURES RELATED TO SUCH LIFE ESTATE DEED.
