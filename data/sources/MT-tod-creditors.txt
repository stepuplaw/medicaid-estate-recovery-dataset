 Montana Code Annotated 2025

 TITLE 72. ESTATES, TRUSTS, AND FIDUCIARY RELATIONSHIPS

 CHAPTER 6. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS ON DEATH

 Part 4. Uniform Real Property Transfer on Death Act

 Liability For Creditor Claims And Statutory Allowances

 72-6-414 . Liability for creditor claims and statutory allowances. A beneficiary of a transfer on death deed is liable for an allowed claim against the transferor's probate estate and statutory allowances to a surviving spouse and children to the extent provided in 72-6-112 .

 History:  En. Sec. 97, Ch. 313, L. 2019.
