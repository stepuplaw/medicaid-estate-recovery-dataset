SB-833 (2015-2016 session)
Chapter Number: 30 (Stats. 2016, ch. 30)
Date Approved by Governor: June 27, 2016
Date Filed with Secretary of State: June 27, 2016

Digest (summarized by WebFetch): the bill would limit claims to circumstances required by federal law and restrict recovery to probate estate assets. It prohibits filing claims against decedents' property if a surviving registered domestic partner exists, and requires waiving claims for modest-value homesteads. The changes apply only to individuals dying on or after January 1, 2017.

Section 22 of the bill amends Welfare and Institutions Code Section 14009.5 (estate claims limited to federally mandated services; "estate" redefined to include only required probate assets; no recovery when a spouse or registered domestic partner survives; hardship waiver for "homesteads of modest value"; interest cap on voluntary postdeath liens; annual statement of recoverable expenses on request).
