 Health Benefits Eligibility and Enrollment Rules (HBEE) 

 The Health Benefits Eligibility and Enrollment (HBEE) rules provide the eligibility standards for Medicaid and other health care programs.

 GCR 22-100: Temporary Adjustments to Premium Assistance under the American Rescue Plan Act 

 Effective January 1, 2021 - December 31, 2025

 Affects the following parts of HBEE:

 Part 2, Section 12.03(a) Applicable tax filer

 Part 7, Section 60.07(b) Applicable percentage table for advanced premium tax credits
