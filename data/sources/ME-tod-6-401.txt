 Part 4: UNIFORM REAL PROPERTY TRANSFER ON DEATH ACT

 §6-402 

 §6-401. Short title

 This Part may be known and cited as "the Uniform Real Property Transfer on Death Act." 

 [PL 2017, c. 402, Pt. A, §2 (NEW); PL 2019, c. 417, Pt. B, §14 (AFF).]
